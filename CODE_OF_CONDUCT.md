# Code of conduct

Gearshift follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short:

- Be welcoming and patient, especially with people who are new to open source or to bb.
- Assume good intent, and critique ideas and code rather than people.
- Disagree in the open and with reasons; accept the maintainer's decision once it's made.
- No harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, sexualised language or imagery,
  or publishing anyone's private information.

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space, and when you
represent the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes the project feel unsafe or unwelcome, tell the maintainer,
[@vanZeben](https://github.com/vanZeben), privately. If you have no private way to reach them,
open an issue that only asks for a private contact, without the details. Reports are handled
confidentially, and the maintainer may remove comments, issues or contributions and block
people who break this code, as the Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.
